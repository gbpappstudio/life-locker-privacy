LIFE LOCKER
OFFLINE FAMILY DOCUMENT VAULT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Life Locker is a completely offline document vault for Android. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information. The short version: nothing you put into Life Locker ever leaves your device unless you personally export a backup file and share it.

What we collect

Nothing. Life Locker has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything off your device over the network.

What you store, and where it lives

Documents, photos, the people you organise them under, categories, tags, expiry dates and asset details are stored only on your device, inside Life Locker's private app storage:

  • The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256).
  • Each imported file is separately encrypted with AES-256-GCM, under a key held in the Android hardware-backed Keystore.
  • Access is gated by a PIN you choose (stored only as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash, never in the clear) and, optionally, your device biometric.

We have no server, so we never receive, see, or store any of this.

Backups

If you choose Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup, Life Locker writes a single password-protected, AES-256-GCM-encrypted .llbackup file to a location you select. From that point the file is under your control and subject to the terms of wherever you saved or sent it. Life Locker cannot read it back without the password you set, and neither can we.

Purchases

The optional one-time "unlock unlimited" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Life Locker never sees your payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The only thing stored on your device as a result is a local flag recording that the unlock is active.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used solely for local, on-device reminders that a document in your vault is approaching its expiry date. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • No internet, no location, no contacts, no background network — none of these are requested.

Children

Life Locker is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Because the app is offline, material changes will accompany an app update.
